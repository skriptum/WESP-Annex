# How to check the tables?

This document explains how to make sure that the outputted tables are similar to the ones in the printed version. Of course you have to adjust the base year in the code for that...



For OECD tables, please make sure you use the provided links or click yourself trough the interface from the start. Old links often times contain a version number, therefore displaying old data! 

## Table 14

Table Name: Total net ODA flows from OECD Development Assistance Committee countries, by type

**All rows except Humanitarian Aid, Loans and LDC rows**

Online table viewer: http://stats.oecd.org/index.aspx?datasetcode=table1

Selection:

- Donor: DAC countries 
- Measure: Official Development Assistance (ODA), and subcategories
 - Bilateral ODA
   - Debt relief
   - Other In-donor expenditures
   - Memo: Free Standing Technical Cooperation
  - Multilateral ODA
    - UN Agencies
    - EU Institutions
    - IDA
    - Other World Bank
    - Regional Development Banks
- Flow type: Disbursements, net
- Price base: Current prices
- Combined unit of measure: US dollar, Millions



**Rows: Humanitarian Aid, Loans and LDC** (other Table!) 

Online table viewer: http://stats.oecd.org/index.aspx?datasetcode=table2a



**Selection for Humanitarian and Loans**

- Donor:  DAC countries
- Recipient: All recipients
- Measure: 
  - Humanitarian aid
  - ODA Loans, net disbursements
  - Debt forgiveness
- Price base: Constant prices
- Combined unit of measure: US dollar, Millions, 2023



**Selection for LDC Row**

- Donor: DAC countries
- Recipient: Least developed countries
- Measure: Official development assistance (ODA), disbursements
- Price base: Constant prices
- Combined unit of measure: US dollar, Millions, 2023



## Table 13

Table Name: Net ODA disbursements from major sources, by type

Online Table viewer: http://stats.oecd.org/index.aspx?datasetcode=table1

**Columns: Growth Rates**

Selection:

- Donor: DAC countries (dont forget the DAC EU countries box at the bottom)
- Measure: Official Development Assistance (ODA), Disbursements net
- Flow type: Disbursements, net
- Price base: Constant
- Combined unit of measure: US dollar, Millions, 2023

**Column: Total ODA**

Selection:

- Donor: DAC countries (dont forget the DAC EU countries box at the bottom)
- Measure: Official Development Assistance (ODA)
- Flow type: Disbursements, net
- Price base: Current
- Combined unit of measure: US dollar, Millions

**Column: Percentage of GNI**

Selection: 

- Donor: DAC countries (dont forget the DAC EU countries box at the bottom)
- Measure: GNI at current prices
- Flow type: Disbursements, net
- Price base: Current
- Combined unit of measure: US dollar, Millions

Then calculate with Column: Total ODA

**Columns: Distribution**

- Donor: DAC countries (dont forget the DAC EU countries box at the bottom)
- Measure: 
  - Bilateral ODA
  - Multilateral ODA
    - UN Agencies
- Flow type: Disbursements, net
- Price base: Current
- Combined unit of measure: US dollar, Millions



## Table 12

Table Name: "Balance of payments on current accounts, by country or country group, summary table"

Online Table (Click "VIEW DATA"): https://data.imf.org/en/datasets/IMF.RES:WEO

Selection:

- Country: All
- Indicator: Current account balance (credit less debit), US dollar
- Frequency: Annual

Check an Example country (e.g Japan) and compare with outputted excel table
